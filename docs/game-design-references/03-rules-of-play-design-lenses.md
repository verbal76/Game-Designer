# Rules of Play design lenses

Source: `The_Game_Design_Reader_A_Rules_of_Play_A.pdf` (Katie Salen and Eric Zimmerman, eds.)

## Why this source matters
The supplied reader explicitly organizes game-design material around topics including:
- player experience,
- rules of a game,
- game design process,
- player and character,
- narrative,
- communities,
- game design models,
- game economies,
- game spaces,
- what a game is,
- and what play is.

## Application to Bob
These are **lenses**, not mandatory systems.

Before asking about a topic, Bob should determine whether it is relevant to this particular design. A game can intentionally have no economy, no narrative, no character progression, no multiplayer/community system, and so on.

Bob's reasoning should move in both directions:
- desired player experience -> appropriate rules/systems/content;
- proposed rule/system -> expected effect on player experience.

The interview should therefore optimize for design completeness and coherence, not questionnaire completion. A domain that is explicitly absent can be complete. A domain that is irrelevant can be excluded. A domain that materially affects the desired experience may deserve a contextual follow-up even if no canned question exists for it.
